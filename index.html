Eggless Cake in Four Flavours (No Oven)

1. Chocolate Cake

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup oil
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  1. Grease a cake tin and line with parchment paper.
  2. Mix flour, cocoa, sugar, and baking powder.
  3. Add milk, oil, and vanilla. Whisk until smooth.
  4. Preheat a heavy-bottomed pan or kadhai with 1 cup salt at the base for 5 minutes.
  5. Place a stand inside, then the cake tin on top. Cover with lid.
  6. Cook on low flame for 35–40 minutes. Check with toothpick.
  7. Cool before serving.

2. Vanilla Cake

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup butter or oil
  • 2 tsp vanilla essence
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  1. Cream sugar and butter until fluffy.
  2. Add milk and vanilla essence.
  3. Fold in flour and baking powder.
  4. Pour into greased tin lined with parchment.
  5. Cook in preheated pan/kadhai on low flame for 30–35 minutes.
  6. Cool before serving.

3. Strawberry Cake

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup strawberry puree
  • ½ cup oil
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Few drops strawberry essence
  1. Mix puree, sugar, and oil.
  2. Add flour, baking powder, and essence.
  3. Pour into greased tin lined with parchment.
  4. Cook in preheated pan/kadhai on low flame for 25–30 minutes.
  5. Cool and decorate with fresh strawberries.

4. Coffee Cake

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup butter or oil
  • 2 tbsp instant coffee (dissolved in warm milk)
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  1. Mix coffee milk with sugar and butter.
  2. Fold in flour and baking powder.
  3. Pour into greased tin lined with parchment.
  4. Cook in preheated pan/kadhai on low flame for 30 minutes.
  5. Cool and dust with cocoa or coffee powder.
